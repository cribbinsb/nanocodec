Yardsticks

A speed figure compared only with our own build says little. Each speed claim on these pages is checked against an independent implementation, named here, and the gaps that check found are listed in the full write-up, worst first.

Which implementation each claim is measured against

Rules of the comparison

  • Every decoder and encoder runs with one thread, including the references, which are normally run multi-threaded and are faster that way.
  • Runs are interleaved and rotated between rounds on a reserved core of a shared PC, so small differences there are noise; the Jetson runs on one core at a locked clock.
  • Our decoders are first checked to produce the same pictures as the reference decoder on every third-party stream used.
  • Where no measurement exists, the write-up says so rather than estimating.
