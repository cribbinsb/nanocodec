Bits against speed

Every nanocodec preset and every reference encoder, each in its own real-time rate control on one CPU core, compared with one zero line. Lower is fewer bits for the same quality; right is faster. Pick the clip set, the rate mode, the metric and the machine. In the 0.6.0 CBR charts nanocodec's buffer is looser than the references': see Rate control and the buffer.

Our own measurements. The score is BD-rate on PSNR-YUV, VMAF v1 and MS-SSIM in equal thirds (J); no subjective test was run. The held-out clips are videos none of our tuning used (most come from the same public collections as the development clips); the development clips are the ones we tune beside and flatter most of our presets. The references are mature, general-purpose encoders measured only at their low-delay, one-thread settings; their other modes may do better. 8-bit 4:2:0 only. The Slow presets are on all three clip sets: both held-out sets measured on 2026-11-15 at build bad14976; the development set at the builds the caption lists.
