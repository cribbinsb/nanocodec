Bits against speed

Every nanocodec preset and every reference encoder of the benchmark procedure, each timed at half load on one machine (x86 or Arm), compared with one zero line, in CBR. Lower is fewer bits for the same quality; right is faster. Pick the timing machine, the dataset and the metric.

Our own measurements. BD-J is the mean of the BD-rates on PSNR-YUV, VMAF v1 and MS-SSIM, each against x264 medium at its default tune; no subjective test was run. Every BD averages every family over the same clips (the common set), except the +drop rows, each over its own clips; the table also gives each family's own anchor-valid clip count. The references are measured only at the low-delay, one-thread settings of the procedure; their other modes may do better.
