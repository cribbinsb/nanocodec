Rate control and the buffer

In the CBR charts every encoder is asked for the same thing: a target bitrate and a one-second buffer, so that a receiver on a channel of that rate never waits more than a second for a frame. This page states what each encoder is given, how full each one's buffer actually gets on the published charts, and the buffer nanocodec keeps from its next release.
