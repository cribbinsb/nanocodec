Spatial scalability (SVC)

One AV1 or VP9 stream carrying the 720p picture and lower-resolution layers under it (360p, and 180p with three layers), so a relay can forward a smaller view without a second encode. Rate is the whole stream, every layer; quality is the 720p output, as BD-rate against single-layer x264 medium. Beside them: the same codec coding 720p alone, simulcast (independent streams per size), and the SVC modes of libaom's and libvpx's real-time example encoders.

    Bits saved at 720p against encode speed

    BD-J of the 720p output at the total stream rate, against single-layer x264 medium · lower is fewer bits, right is faster

    Every point

    Lower operating points: each view a receiver can take, as BD-J against the same codec coding that view alone (the simulcast streams of that size), at that view's own rate. The 720p receiver view counts only the bits a 720p receiver downloads (in K-SVC modes, _KEY, the lower layers only on key frames) against the codec's single-layer 720p.
