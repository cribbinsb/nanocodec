The clips

Every clip each benchmark dataset coded: its size, rate, windows and the rungs the shared ladder gives it, and, for the chosen timing machine, which encoders' curves on it were malformed. Names and numbers only: no frame or stream is published.

nanocodec is built on the work of SVT-AV1, libaom, dav1d, libvpx, openh264, kvazaar, VVenC / VVdeC, AVM, the JM / HM / VTM reference software and the standards' authors; x264, x265, SVT-AV1, libaom, libvpx, openh264, Kvazaar and VVenC are its comparison points here. With thanks to all of them.
