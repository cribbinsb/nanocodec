# x86 without AVX-512 (AWS c6a.large, Zen 3) speed series (2026-11-17)

The results site has a fourth speed machine, "x86 without AVX-512 (Zen 3)", beside the Jetson, Zen 5 (c8a) and
Graviton4 (c8g). On it every encoder runs its AVX2 code. It covers both charts:

- **Fixed QP (AOM CTC lens):** [fixed-qp-timing-zen3.json](fixed-qp-timing-zen3.json).
- **CBR charts (the speed axis of all three CBR views):** [bd-vs-speed-timing-zen3.json](bd-vs-speed-timing-zen3.json).
- **Not timed here:** the held-out Fixed QP view (private clips).

## Method

- **Machine.** AWS c6a.large: AMD EPYC 7R13 (Zen 3), eu-west-1, on demand. Its two vCPUs are the two SMT threads of
  one core, unlike c8a and c8g (no SMT): the timed thread runs on vCPU 1, beside the system's vCPU 0 (mostly idle
  while timing). The platform note on the site says so.
- **SIMD.** `/proc/cpuinfo` lists avx2 and no avx512 flag.
  - Our binary refuses `NC_DSP_ISA=avx512` ("not supported by this CPU/build"). In the pipe-clean, H.264 Fast on the
    default tier and with AVX2 forced coded the same streams on the 3 chart clips at the same time (0.962x and 0.972x
    M13's time).
  - SVT-AV1: "asm level selected: up to avx2". M13 with `--asm avx2` coded M13's streams at 0.992x its time.
  - x264 and x265 report "... FMA3 BMI2 AVX2".
- **Our encoder.** One x86 release build of variance-integration 2ec3255c (the outlined-intrinsic fix 62400d808 and
  its CI check; `tools/ci/check_outlined_simd.py`: 0 outlined SIMD functions), built as chart_refresh's timed
  release build. Later landings (ffb012fdc, 5af6aaa07) are not in it.
- **Like for like with Zen 5.** Our encoders were re-timed on c8a.large at the same build, the same day, with the
  same jobs (Fixed QP: `fixedqp_timing.py plan-from`; CBR: `aws_timing.py`). The c8a columns below are that
  re-time for our points and the site's stored c8a records for the references (the same reference binaries).
  The site's own c8a series is unchanged (its chart builds).
- **References.** The x86 builds the c8a series runs: SVT-AV1 4.3.0, x264 0.165, x265 4.2, libvpx 1.16.0, libaom
  3.15.3, openh264, HM, VVenC. Per encode, their lens streams equal the c8a series' byte for byte.
- **Fixed QP lens.** `fixedqp_timing.py plan --machine zen3 --thin hm-ldp:27,42`, `run`, `record`: 45 encoders x 12
  sequences x 6 quantizers (HM at 2), 3192 encodes on 32 instances; c8a re-time of our 25 encoders, 1800 encodes on
  8 instances. Rounds as on c8a: 3 rotated rounds after a warm-up below 20 s estimated, else 1, median kept.
  - Our 1800 lens streams are byte-identical on c6a and c8a. AV1 Faster's commands differ from the stored c8a lens
    (the preset changed since b11b2f83); all other lens encodes match it.
  - Round-to-round spread (largest deviation from the median, encodes of 0.2 s or more): c6a median 1.09 %, 90th
    percentile 7.18 % (2447 encodes); c8a re-time 0.65 % / 4.64 %. The Graviton4 series had 0.37 % / 1.20 %.
- **CBR chart timing.** `aws_timing.py --instance-type c6a.large`: all 43 plan points on the 3 chart clips, paired
  in 11 rotated rounds with SVT-AV1 M13 (and x264 medium), one pair at a time; 8 sessions side by side (one re-run:
  x265 slow's pairs outlast the 5-minute stall limit, now 15 minutes for the timing stage). Merged with
  `anchor_chart_timing.py merge --build 2ec3255c`. Every point's streams equal the plan's.
  - Instance-to-instance difference: M13's paired speed per session ranged 217.8-237.1 fps on the 8 c6a sessions
    (8.8 %), 449.9-453.6 on the 3 c8a sessions (0.8 %).
- **Cost.** $1.16 in the ledger for these sessions (c6a and c8a), all terminated.

## Positions against SVT-AV1 M10 / M12 / M13

"Ahead of" means faster and fewer bits (the site's BD values, unchanged), both at once. Quality is the same on both
machines; only the speed axis differs.

- **Fixed QP (Weighted and J): the same on both machines.** Weighted: AV1 Veryfast ahead of M13. J: AV1 Fast ahead of
  M10, AV1 Veryfast ahead of M13. Nothing else of ours is ahead of an SVT point on either.
- **CBR, held-out fresh view (J against x264 medium --tune psnr).**
  - On Zen 5: HEVC Fast, AV1 Fast, AV1 Faster, AV1 Veryfast and VP9 Faster ahead of M10; AV1 Veryfast also ahead of
    M12.
  - On Zen 3, all of these hold, plus: VP9 Fast and AV2 Fast ahead of M10; HEVC Faster ahead of M12 and M13; AV1
    Veryfast also ahead of M13.
  - Some of the Zen 3 margins are within the 8.8 % instance-to-instance difference above: HEVC Faster against M13
    (231.5 against 230.8 fps, 0.3 %), AV1 Veryfast against M13 (232.9 against 230.8, 0.9 %), AV2 Fast against M10
    (117.7 against 115.0, 2.3 %), HEVC Faster against M12 (231.5 against 219.7, 5.4 %), VP9 Fast against M10
    (125.2 against 115.0, 8.9 %).
- **CBR, all held-out view:** the same additions on Zen 3; there HEVC Faster is ahead of M10 on both machines.
- **Speed ratios behind it (measured, not a cause):** c6a/c8a is 0.50 for M10, 0.54 for M12 and 0.52 for M13 on the
  CBR clips (0.52-0.54 on the lens); our points 0.53-0.63 (0.52-0.65 on the lens).
