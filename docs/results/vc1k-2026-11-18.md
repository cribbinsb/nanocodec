# VC-1K measurement set (2026-11-18)

A new measurement dataset for the results site: phone video calls from Meta's
[VC-1K](https://github.com/facebookresearch/VC-1K) (CC BY-NC 4.0), measured under our CBR chart conditions and
the held-out Fixed QP view's conditions.

**Licence and privacy.** VC-1K is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0, and 899 of its clips show participants' faces. We
publish numbers and clip names only: never frames, thumbnails, crops or streams of these clips. Credit:
"VC-1K (Meta, CC BY-NC 4.0)", github.com/facebookresearch/VC-1K. Encoding and scoring ran on our own rented AWS
machines, which counts as processing, not distribution. The tooling deletes every remote copy when a session
ends.

## Plan (written before the runs)

- the 2026-11-15 held-out encode: 13,156 encodes of ~0.5 Mpx clips in 2.5 min on one c7a.metal-48xl;
- the 0.3.0 release's cloud scoring (2026-11-18): 6-7 points/min per 8-worker shard, 96 shards per machine,
  ~0.5 Mpx;
- the 2026-11-16 held-out Fixed QP view: 12 clips x 38 families in 35 min of this PC at 8 workers, ~0.49 Mpx,
  and its c8a timing in 8 + 13 min wall on 10 instances;
- the release's site publish: 5.3 min.

VC-1K clips are 0.92 Mpx, about 1.9x those.

## Clip selection (S1)

`eval/clipset/select_vc1k.py` (seed `vc1k-2026-11-18`) wrote the `build_pool.py` selection
`eval/clipset/vc1k.json`. The clip set is `~/nanocodec-data/clips/vc1k_2026-11-18.json`; its per-stratum counts,
mix and Fixed QP sample are recorded in the selection and the clip set.

- **Candidates.** The 930 even-frame-rate clips (`all_even_fps.txt`); the 77 jitter-fps clips are not used. Five
  clips were left out by the build's checks, each with its reason in `eval/clipset/vc1k_excluded.json`:
  - three where the scene-cut check fired at 0.0-0.1 s;
  - two with fewer than 150 frames in the excerpt (uneven source timestamps).
- **Strata.** Motion (slow/medium/fast) x lighting (dark/low/medium/good) x environment (indoor/outdoor): 24
  strata.
  - Each stratum is filled evenly by water filling: a stratum smaller than the even share (4) gives all its clips.
  - The remainder goes one each to strata in a fixed hash order.
  - Within a stratum, the device x orientation groups are taken in turn.
- **Stored form.** The same as the fresh held-out set's clips:
  - 165 frames from 2.0 s (MEASURED_FRAMES 150 + 15 spare), at the native displayed size (the phones' rotation
    applied: 72 portrait 720x1280, 28 landscape 1280x720);
  - x264 CRF 16, capped at 8 Mbps, 1 s keys.
  - The source rate of each clip is the original file's video bit rate, scaled to the stored size (1.5-17.4
    Mbps; median 11.8 Mbps across all 930 clips).
- **≤ 50 % of source.** Every ladder rung is checked against each clip's own source rate (`Clip.allows_kbps`).
  - 99 clips keep all four rungs (150-1200 kbps at 720p30, pixel-rate scaled).
  - One (`vc1k_android_indoor_slowmotion_darklight_1280x720_30_3688244638`, 1,460 kbps source) drops the
    1200 kbps rung.

## Fixed QP view (S3)

- **Clips.** The 12-clip sample (`vc1k_qp_2026-11-18.json`): one clip per motion x lighting cell, the
  environment alternating; 11 portrait 720x1280 and one landscape 1280x720.
- **Quality.** `nceval.fixedqp gap --clipset ... --clips 12` on this PC, every encoder of the held-out fresh view.
  - Ours: release 0.3.0's encoder (b33ba3bb) with its own presets (`--presets-dir`, new).
  - AV1 Veryfast is included here; the 2026-11-16 fresh view lacked it.
  - AV2 Faster was added after the release: it runs on d7a605aa (the encoder of variance-integration 228579fd)
    with that commit's presets.
  - J against x264 medium at fixed QP, with a 90 % bootstrap interval over clips. Data:
    [fixed-qp-vc1k-2026-11-18.json](fixed-qp-vc1k-2026-11-18.json).
- **Speed.** The same encodes timed on c8a.large (`fixedqp_timing.py plan-pool`, 16 + 2 instances):
  [fixed-qp-vc1k-timing-x86.json](fixed-qp-vc1k-timing-x86.json). Not timed on the Jetson.
- **A fault fixed on the way.** `nceval.fixedqp gap` gave our families the tree's presets whatever the binary.
  The first VC-1K run therefore ran AV2 Fast with the tree's newer preset (temporal layers, sub-pel walk) on the
  release binary. AV2 Fast was re-run with the release's own presets: J −37.1 against −36.9 under the tree's
  preset. Both presets show the same large drop from AV2 Default (−49.6), so the drop is not this fault.
  `gap` and `plan-pool` now take `--presets-dir`.

## CBR chart (S2)

- **What ran.** Every chart family on its main-chart build (`bd-vs-speed-vc1k_2026-11-18-builds.json`, a copy of
  the main chart's record), plus AV2 Faster on d7a605aa. The conditions are the chart's: 150 frames, one key,
  low-delay CBR, ladder within 50 % of each clip's source, low rungs where curves need them.
  - Coding: 23,920 + 598 encodes on rented machines (`aws_heldout_chart.py run --record`).
  - Scoring: XPSNR on, in cloud sessions (`score-cloud --record --session`).
  - Rendered here against both zero lines.
- **Results.** [bd-vs-speed-vc1k_2026-11-18-vs-x264-medium-psnr.md](bd-vs-speed-vc1k_2026-11-18-vs-x264-medium-psnr.md)
  (the site's view) and [bd-vs-speed-vc1k_2026-11-18.md](bd-vs-speed-vc1k_2026-11-18.md).
  - 98 of the 100 clips are scored; two dark clips have no reliable overlap for some points.
- **Speed axis.** The main chart's timing on its own clips, as for the held-out charts. AV2 Faster has no chart
  timing yet (it is newer than the release), so it has no speed.
- **Not included.** The metric-tuned twins (x265 `--tune psnr`, etc.) were not coded on this set
  (`render --tuned ZERO`).

**Against the plan.** The total was ~3.5 h against ~100 min planned:

- Cloud scoring ran at ~100 points/min per c7a.metal, against the ~360 planned (720p points cost about 3.5x

- eu-west-1 had no c7a.48xlarge capacity, so two clip groups shared one machine until 16:10.
- The Fixed QP gap took 83 min against 25-35 min on the loaded PC.
- Adding AV1 Veryfast and AV2 Faster, and the preset fix, cost ~25 min.
