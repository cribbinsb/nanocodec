# Held-out set heldout_2026-11-23: card

A sealed held-out chart set, fresh by **source collection** and not just by file. It answers
finding 8 of `docs/analysis/results-audit-2026-11-23.md`: the earlier set `independent` was fresh
per file, but 113 of its 150 clips came from the development datasets' directories. It had been
re-measured at seven or more builds, and one sweep tuned `rate.key_cap_buffer` on it.

- Selection: `eval/clipset/select_heldout.py` (seed `heldout_2026-11-23`) writes
  `eval/clipset/heldout_2026-11-23.json`. `build_pool.py` and `grade.py --no-sheets` build and
  grade it, and `select_heldout.py finalize` keeps the final clips. The kept clip set is
  `~/nanocodec-data/clips/heldout_2026-11-23.json`, with its clips in
  `~/nanocodec-data/clips/heldout_2026-11-23/`. Every drawn clip's outcome is in
  `eval/clipset/heldout_2026-11-23.outcome.json`.
- Sealed: release and published-chart measurement only (`nceval.sealing`; docs/PROCEDURES.md
  "Sealed held-out sets").
- Measurements so far: 0.

**Kept: 100 clips from 100 families.** Per class: bodycam 15, drone 15, static 15, texture 15,

- 9 were reserves not needed.

**Weak classes, stated plainly:** pan (3 clips) and crowd (10) are thin. Read them as part of
the whole set, not as per-class results. For the next draw: store a grade-B source 2× down
instead of refusing it, which would bring back the Caltech drives at 320×240.

## Rules (fixed in the script; nobody picks clips)

- Each source runs at 10 to 120 fps. It is long enough for 150 measured frames plus 15 spare,
  after 2 s. Its start time is drawn from the seed anywhere the excerpt fits.
- A source the pool's grading could accept (`select_independent.stored_height`) is stored at its
  native size when its bits per pixel reach `nceval.pool.BPP_FLOOR`, and 2× down otherwise.
  `grade.py` then grades the stored clip, and grade C or a B under the pool's rule is refused.
- Stored heights rotate over 720, 540 and 360 within each class, capped by what the source
  allows and never upscaled. The draw's (not the kept set's) heights: 540 lines 84, 360 lines 51, 720 lines 19,
  240 lines 13 (UCF-Crime's native 320×240), 480 lines 8 (Caltech's native 640×480).
  Source sizes run from 320×240 to 3840×2160, including 9 portrait sources.
- Clips are stored as `build_pool.py` camera encodes: H.264, CRF 16, capped at 8 Mbps per 720p,
  1 s closed GOPs, cut before any scene cut. Each clip's source rate is the lower of the stored
  rate and the original's rate scaled to the stored pixel rate.
- Rate ladders: the chart's real-use ladder (`nceval.realuse.LADDER_KBPS` 150/300/600/1200 kbps
  at 720p30, scaled per clip) and its low rungs. Each rung is capped at 50 % of the clip's source
  rate (`nceval.cbr.clip_targets`), as on every chart set.

## Disjointness evidence

`select_heldout.py used` writes `eval/clipset/heldout_2026-11-23.used.json`. It scans:

- every commit on every ref of the repository (`git log --all -p`, excluding this selection's
  own files);
- 13 701 JSON, text, Markdown, CSV, TOML and JSONL files under the data root's `clips/` (every
  clip set ever written there), `nn-data/` (the learned-search datasets) and `tuning/`.

It reports:

- **46 `/mldata` source directories used**, at dataset level, with mention counts. They are every
  `tracking_original` dataset (antare_bwc, bdd100k_mot, bwc-videotext, cevo, cevo_april25,
  chirla, jaad, meva, mot, otw, personpath22, raw_movies, roundabouthd, uvg_vcm); the
  `/mldata/video` directories and files (answer_suite, antare, base, cctv, mp4, youtube, the
  MOT20, bc1/bc2 and cam22 files); and in `downloaded_datasets/other`: ABODA, CAUCAFall, CHAD,
  FIRESENSE, GMDCSA24, JAAD, MEVA, otw, personpath22, towncentre, vc1k and WILDTRACK. Five
  mentions name only the bare directory `downloaded_datasets/other` and no dataset under it.
- **Zero mentions of every candidate collection**, both as its path and as its name tokens
  (whole-word: "vcd2" inside a "vvcD2" profile file name does not count). `select` refuses to
  run if any collection has a mention.
- Other sets: the sets that do not come from `/mldata` (xiph, svt, blender, the standards lens's
  CTC sequences, adv12) share no collection with these. Bath's RoundaboutHD (`bath_1574`) and
  the Antare downloads were left out because they duplicate used datasets.

At file level, too, none of the 175 sources appears in any other clip set. The scan covered the
same files.

Limits, stated plainly:

- Publishers: VCD2 and VC-1K both come from Microsoft's video-call research. They are different
  datasets and different captures (webcams in VCD2, phone calls in VC-1K).
- Classes: each class comes from the collection's kind of camera, not from measured motion. The
  Wikimedia categories are uploads, and the scene-cut rule removes most edited content.
- Coverage: the 720-line clips are few, because most 1080p web sources fall under the bits-per-pixel
  floor at 720p and are stored at 540 lines.

## Sealing

- `nceval.sealing.RELEASE_ONLY_SETS` lists the set. `nceval.clips.load_clipset` refuses it,
  whatever its file name, unless `NC_RELEASE_MEASURE=1` is set by hand on a release or publish
  command line.
- `nceval.pool.SEALED_SETS` includes it, so its families never enter the screening pool or the
  learned-search data. Those reads use `exclusion_only` loads.
- `tools/ci/check_sealed_sets.py`, run by `check.sh`, refuses code outside its allow-list that
  names the set, sets the switch, or makes an `exclusion_only` load.
- `aws_heldout_chart.py` forwards the switch to its rented boxes (`sealing.remote_env()`).
