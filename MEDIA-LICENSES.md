# Sample media licences

nanocodec's BSD-3-Clause licence ([LICENSE](LICENSE)) covers nanocodec's source code and documentation. It does not
cover the sample media in web/clips/ (video clips) or web/streams-ref/
(streams coded from them): they keep their rights holders' own licences, some
NonCommercial or ShareAlike, listed in web/clips/ATTRIBUTION.md and
docs/LEGAL.md.
