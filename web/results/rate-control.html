Coding efficiency and rate control

A CBR-against-CBR comparison mixes two things: how well each encoder codes, and how much its real-time rate control costs it. This run measures them apart, on the development clips: each encoder's CBR curve and a fixed-QP curve over the same rates, for our Default presets and for x264 medium at both tunings.

Development clips, one build. These are the clips we tune beside, not the held-out set, and the run has no low rungs. A clip counts only where the curves overlap reliably: the number of such clips is in brackets. In this low-delay mode (one-second buffer, no lookahead) x264's own rate control is expensive for it, which is part of why our CBR margins are larger than our fixed-QP ones.
