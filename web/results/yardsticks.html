Yardsticks

A speed figure compared only with our own build says little. Each speed claim on these pages is checked against an independent implementation, named here, and the gaps that check found are listed in the full write-up, worst first.

Rules of the comparison

  • Every decoder and encoder runs with one thread, including the references, which are normally run multi-threaded and are faster that way.
  • x86 encode times come from a rented AWS c8a.large (AMD EPYC 9R45), one session per batch of points; ours are scaled per clip to an earlier session's x264 medium time, so points of different sessions share one axis. The Jetson runs on one core at a locked clock.
  • Our decoders are first checked to produce the same pictures as the reference decoder on every third-party stream used.
  • Where no measurement exists, the write-up says so rather than estimating.

Read the measurements

  • Speed claims against independent yardsticks: every claim, its yardstick, the measurements and the worst gaps.
  • Decoder speed: our decoders against FFmpeg and dav1d, timed in process on x86, natively and in the browser (2026-11-23).
  • Speed: the encode, browser, decoder and hardware tables, generated from the repository's data.
