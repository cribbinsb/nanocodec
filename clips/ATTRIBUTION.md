# Sample clip attribution

The demo's sample clips are short excerpts of Blender Foundation open movies,
released under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>). They are redistributed here
under that licence; the excerpts carry the same licence.

Changes made to every clip: cut to the segment below, audio removed, and
re-encoded with ffmpeg/libx264 as 1280×720 H.264 High (yuv420p, 24 fps,
`-preset slower`, MP4 with faststart) for browser playback. The Tears of Steel
excerpts were also centre-cropped from 2.4:1 (1920×800) to 16:9 (1422×800)
before scaling.
